### Vedic daily calculation layer

Added a deterministic, offline Vedic daily indicator core that accepts explicit tropical Sun/Moon longitudes and an explicit versioned ayanamsha value. The implementation:

- normalizes tropical minus ayanamsha into sidereal longitudes,
- divides the zodiac into 27 Nakshatras,
- divides each Nakshatra into 4 Padas,
- derives 30 Tithis from 12-degree Moon−Sun elongation,
- separates Shukla and Krishna Paksha,
- preserves ephemeris provenance separately from ayanamsha provenance,
- rejects non-finite inputs and empty provenance.

Boundary tests cover 0°, Nakshatra transition, 359.999999°, Tithi 15→16 Paksha transition and provenance failure. A `vedicIndicator` DailySnapshot adapter, source-level manifest, validator and dedicated CI workflow were added.
